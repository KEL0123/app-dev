## 📌 Overview
**Need for Speed** is an action-thriller film adapted from the iconic street-racing video game franchise. It follows street racer and mechanic **Tobey Marshall** as he sets out on a cross-country race to avenge his friend and clear his name.

## ⭐ Why It’s My Favorite
* **Practical Stunts:** Real supercars were used instead of heavy CGI.
* **Engine Sounds:** Authentic audio of modified V8 engines and custom exhausts.
* **Brotherhood & Redemption:** A solid story about loyalty and perseverance.
